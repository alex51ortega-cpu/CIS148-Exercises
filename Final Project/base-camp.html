Everest Base Camp

Sitting at an astounding altitude of approximately 17,598 feet (5,364 meters), the South Base Camp in Nepal serves as the central nervous system for every single commercial climbing expedition on Mount Everest. For weeks at a time, this bustling tent city on top of the moving Khumbu Glacier becomes home to hundreds of climbers, guides, and support staff, providing a crucial environment for essential high-altitude acclimatization.

Life at Base Camp requires a delicate balance of physical rest, continuous gear preparation, and logistical synchronization. Teams spend their days practicing navigation through the treacherous lower icefall, checking oxygen systems, and waiting for the highly anticipated weather windows that unlock safe passages toward the higher camp clusters and the ultimate summit ridge.
