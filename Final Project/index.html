Welcome to the Ascent

Mount Everest, standing at a majestic 29,031.7 feet (8,848.86 meters) above sea level, is the highest mountain on Earth. Located in the Mahalangur Himal sub-range of the Himalayas, the international border between Nepal and China runs across its summit point. This guide provides comprehensive info for climbers trying to tackle the peak.

Whether you are exploring the routes, learning about the crucial role of the Sherpa guides, or studying the dangerous realities of the Death Zone, this resource is designed to give you a detailed breakdown of what it takes to survive and conquer the roof of the world.
